East County Cruisers Car Show and Toy Drive

Event: East County Cruisers Car Show and Toy Drive

Date: Saturday, November 7, 2026

Time: 9:00am to 2:00pm

Location: Bates Nut Farm

Address: 15954 Woods Valley Road, Valley Center, CA

Website: http://www.EastCountyCruisers.com

Description: The Annual East County Cruisers Car Show & Toy Drive will be back at Bates Nut Farm this year! Bring your family and friends to the farm, and check out the classic cars on display from 9:00am to 2:00pm. Complete your act of kindness for the day by bringing a new, unwrapped toy for the toy drive. Toys and proceeds benefit local military families for the holidays. For more information, or to register your car for the show, visit www.eastcountycruisers.com. Food and music too!

Start your holiday shopping early at the Christmas on the Farm - Craft Fair also happening at Bates Nut Farm on Saturday and Sunday, 11/7 & 11/8, from 9am - 4pm!

Free parking and admission.
