Sweet Pea Day

Saturday April 18, 2026

Spring is in!

Note: Due to early hot weather, our sweet pea flower blooms are low in production.

Come out to the Farm and walk through our towering fragrant maze of these old-fashioned, dainty flowers! Children love it and great photo opportunities! Then, cut and make your own Sweet Pea bouquet! Fee: $5.00, instructions included (during Business Hours only, availability may vary).

Summer Events

Lavender Day & Antique Craft Fair

Saturday June 20, 2026

9:00am-3:00pm

Come celebrate the fragrance and beauty of lavender. Spend the day with a friend at the farm and shop in the barn for great lavender gifts like lavender soaps, candles and linen spray. (My favorite product is still the Lavender Flower Garden Salve!) Lavender herbal soaps fill the soap shop with the scent of the south of France ~ come and experience it.

Fill your garden and containers with many types of lavender. Remember to wear lavender!

Over 40 fine craftsman and expert artisans can be found at this event. Crafts, collectables, antiques, and a garden sale all on our spacious lawn area. Enjoy your day shopping at this great outdoor event.

The Solana Center will be offering the community resources and information on composting, upcoming workshops, how to properly dispose of different waste products, as well as providing a variety of helpful literature about local resources.
