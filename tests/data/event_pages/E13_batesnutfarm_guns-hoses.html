8th Annual Guns & Hoses Chili Cook-Off Fundraiser

Event: 8th Annual Guns & Hoses Chili Cook-Off Fundraiser

Date: Sunday, November 8, 2026

Time: 12:00pm - 3:00pm

Location: Bates Nut Farm

Address: 15954 Woods Valley Road, Valley Center, CA

Phone: 760-749-3333

Description:  Join local first responders for the 8th annual Guns & Hoses Chili Cook-off. Who can cook up the best chili? - who has your vote? Sheriff, CHP, Fire/Ambulance, and other groups! Come support this fundraiser for local fire explorer programs.

Normally held over Western Days, we rescheduled the event this year to November. Come enjoy entertainment and the Bates craft show! 

This event is present by Valley Center Business Association.
