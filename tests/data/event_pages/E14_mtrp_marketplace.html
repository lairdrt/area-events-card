Holiday Marketplace Benefiting Mission Trails Regional Park

 

Shop local this holiday season at Mission Trails Regional Park!

Do your holiday shopping at Mission Trails. Shop local. Meet the makers. Spend the day somewhere beautiful and support the park.

The annual Holiday Marketplace brings local makers and vendors together for a weekend of holiday shopping at the Mission Trails Regional Park Visitor Center. Shop for one-of-a-kind gifts from local artists and small businesses, meet the makers behind them and enjoy a day at one of San Diego’s most beautiful places.

Visitors can explore the Visitor Center, take in the views and make a Mission Trails outing out of their holiday shopping.

Best of all, a portion of each vendor’s gross sales goes directly to the Mission Trails Regional Park Foundation (MTRP Foundation), helping care for and enhance Mission Trails Regional Park.

When you shop the Holiday Marketplace, you’re supporting local makers while giving back to the park you love.

2026 HOLIDAY MARKETPLACE

Dates: Saturday, November 14 and Sunday, November 15, 2026
Time: 9 a.m.–3 p.m. both days
Location: Mission Trails Regional Park Visitor Center
One Father Junipero Serra Trail
San Diego, CA 92119

The event is free but please register here in advance.

2026 VENDORS

This year’s Holiday Marketplace will feature local artists, makers, bakers and small businesses offering ceramics, jewelry, art, baked goods, home decor, paper goods, woodworking and more.

Come ready to discover something you won’t find at the mall and meet the people who made it.

Participating vendors include:

  • Bradbury-Bennett
  • Candlefy
  • Chrysanthemum Art
  • Folk Project
  • Ingrained West Woodworks
  • Jodie Talmadge Woodburned Art
  • Juliana Tipton Design
  • Muxu Creations
  • Olly’s Baked Assortments (O.B.A.)
  • Open Studio
  • Out West Ceramics
  • Raíz Chocolate
  • SAVAGE BEE-CHES
  • San Diego Sourdough Bakery
  • Sea to Summit Ceramics
  • Two Hermanas

PARKING + SHUTTLE

Parking at the Mission Trails Regional Park Visitor Center fills quickly, so Holiday Marketplace visitors are encouraged to use our satellite parking and free shuttle.

Satellite parking will be available at the southernmost end of the Keil’s Food Store shopping center parking lot in San Carlos, near the intersection of Jackson Drive and Navajo Road.

Please park between Ace Hardware and Primos Mexican Food and take the shuttle to the Mission Trails Regional Park Visitor Center.

Please do not park directly in front of Keil’s Food Store or any of the other storefronts. Those spaces need to remain available for customers of the businesses in the shopping center.

Make a day of it: shop local, meet the makers, enjoy Mission Trails and support the park.
