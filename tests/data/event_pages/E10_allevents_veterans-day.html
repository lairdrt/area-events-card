About the event

Veterans Day Program
🇺🇸 CCHS would like to invite all Veterans and the community to join us for our Annual Veterans Day Celebration on November 6 at 2:00 p.m. in the Jets Auditorium. We look forward to honoring those who have served!

Veterans, please arrive in the auditorium lobby by 1:45 p.m. ❤️🤍💙🇺🇸
