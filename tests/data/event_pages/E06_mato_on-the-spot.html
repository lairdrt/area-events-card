On The Spot
Dating 1-Year Party

When
Saturday, 7 November
17:00 - 22:00
About

On The Spot Dating marks one year of San Diego singles getting out of their comfort zones with an anniversary party at AleSmith Brewing Company. The evening features a live dating show, a social mixer, an after-party, and special surprises, perks, and giveaways. Doors open at 5 PM, the dating show begins at 6 PM, and the event runs until 10 PM. Tickets are available through the link in the organizer's bio; the same link can be used to apply to be a participant on stage.
