2026 Seven Oaks Holiday Craft Fair

An Annual Holiday Shopping Event Featuring 

Handmade Products From Local Artists and Artistans


Sewn Goods - Potted Plants - Jewelry - Fine Art - Ceramics - Toys - Woodworking - Soaps - Edible Gifts - Candles - Affordable Opening Prices - Gifts For Everyone on Your List

Saturday - Dec. 5, 2026

9am to 3pm

Location: Seven Oaks Community Center 

16789 Bernardo Oaks Drive

San Diego, California 92128

in Rancho Bernardo

The Seven Oaks Community Center in Rancho Bernardo, 16789 Bernardo Oaks Dr., San Diego 92128

  • The Seven Oaks Community Center Annual Holiday Craft Fair is a free event open to the public. Families welcome. Please leave pets at home. 

  • Find hundreds of gifts from local makers, bakers and artists.

  • Held each year in Rancho Bernardo, 16789 Bernardo Oaks Drive, San Diego 92128. Plenty of free lot and street parking

  • New fair format is bigger than in previous years, with many more vendors.

  • Seven Oaks is Rancho Bernardo's original 55+ planned community. The annual Holiday Craft Fair is held in the Seven Oaks Community Center. About 25% of vendors are Seven Oaks residents and these grandmas (and grandpas) make the best gifts.
